# Terminal companion

The white scout companion is the terminal's animated player sidekick. Open the terminal to reveal his head, chest and arms; close it with a target selected to dock him with approximately half his head visible and his eyes asleep. Clearing the target hides the docked character. Opening the chart, Log, Settings or the main menu hides him so those interfaces remain clear.

His body language reacts locally to terminal input, scanning and completed records, clearing the screen, travel and warp, arrivals, first landings, collected samples and warnings. Quiet moments include breathing, blinking, glancing and stretching. Closed-terminal reactions stay asleep. This update adds animation and body language; it does not introduce a remote AI service or change terminal commands.

The avatar follows the visible terminal pocket's top edge during its slide. It scales down with the space above an enlarged terminal and avoids the camera rectangles and nearby controls. It never intercepts input. Reduced motion stops breathing, waving, thruster pulses, sparkles and the reveal transition while keeping readable expressions. The raster atlas is downsampled once to small canvas parts and animated at no more than 20 painted frames per second.

## Artwork

`assets/terminal-companion.png` is the original transparent 1254 × 1254 output from the built-in image-generation tool, based on the user's attached white scout reference. The head, chest and two arms are separate atlas components. Their measured alpha bounds are recorded in `src/ui/terminal-companion.js`; the dark visor receives code-drawn expressions. The original PNG is preserved and precached for offline play.
