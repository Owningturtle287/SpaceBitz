# Terminal companion

The terminal scout is now a white head with two floating, softly tapered arms. There is no chest, torso, finger, elbow or wrist articulation. Each arm has one circular shoulder socket, mounted beside the lower head; the complete rigid paddle swings around that socket. Closed-terminal mode exposes the upper sleeping head while the terminal hides the lower face and arms.

## Sprite animation

The transparent atlas has six distinct head drawings: forward neutral, sleep inhale, sleep exhale, alert, left glance and right glance. They share a 52 × 44 pixel head frame with visor-aligned offsets, preserving their individual artwork instead of stretching a front sprite into a turn. Ten expression variants are baked for each drawing on load. Sleeping eyes are two flat seven-pixel lines, each one pixel high.

Each left/right arm is rasterized once into twenty-nine crisp shoulder-angle sprites, from −140° to +140° in 10° steps. Runtime painting selects those frames and never bends an elbow, moves a hand independently or rotates the display canvas. The shoulder stays mounted at the head's lower side. The arm tip follows one constant-radius arc. Greets wave the right arm while the left rests; completed records use a left presenting gesture; celebrations and stretches raise both arms; clearing makes one shoulder sweep. Wake opens the eyes in successive flat, half-open, open and wide frames. Event sequences include anticipation, a gesture hold or repeated wave, and a return to rest. Shoulder speed is bounded so interrupted gestures settle without teleporting.

The renderer is **84 × 60 native pixels**, uses fourteen flat colors, nearest-neighbor sampling and binary alpha, and paints at up to 30 frames per second. The head begins three rows below the canvas top; breathing moves the entire head and its shoulder mounts by at most one row. No head is drawn at a negative y-coordinate. Only the stage's lower edge masks the sleeping face, so the top outline and crest remain intact. Sleeping inhale/exhale drawings also fold and relax the antenna.

## Placement and behavior

The sprite scale comes only from viewport height, capped at the previous closed-terminal scale: 2.0625 CSS pixels per native pixel at 390px landscape height. Opening the terminal changes the reveal window from 32 to 52 rows, without changing its width or canvas scale. Small viewports use the same smaller scale in both modes. Enlarging the terminal bounds the reveal window to the available space rather than shrinking the head.

The avatar follows the visible terminal pocket's top edge, avoids the camera rectangles and nearby controls, and never intercepts input. It hides for the chart, Log, Settings and main menu. Reactions remain local and cosmetic: input, scans, completed records, clearing, travel, warp, arrivals, landings, samples and warnings. Sleeping mode ignores action reactions. Reduced motion freezes breathing, sprite cycling and arm motion while retaining readable expressions. Game commands, saves and flight behavior are unchanged.

## Artwork

`assets/terminal-companion.png` is the unmodified **1774 × 887** transparent atlas from the built-in image-generation tool in edit mode, replacing the previous head/chest/hand atlas. Its measured regions are recorded in `src/ui/terminal-companion.js`. The six head poses and two single-piece arms are downsampled and converted to the game's palette at load. The atlas and animation module are included in the offline shell.
